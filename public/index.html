Inventory is kept in this tab's temporary filesystem. Restarting or refreshing starts fresh.


Try an example
  1. Choose 1 (Add Item), then 1 (Add). Enter Rice, 80, 10.
  2. Choose 1 again. Enter Sugar, 45, 8.
  3. Choose 2 to return to the main menu, then 2 (Print Bill).
  4. Choose 1 (Add Bill). Enter Rice, 2.
  5. Choose 1 again. Enter Sugar, 3.
  6. Choose 2 (Close Session). The total is 295.
  7. Choose 3 at the main menu to exit.
